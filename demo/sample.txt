Maat keeps the editing loop quiet and explicit.

The buffer carries its own SHA-256 fingerprint.
Before a save, Maat weighs it against the file on disk.
An external modification becomes a visible conflict, not silent data loss.

As above, so below: memory and disk must agree.

Try these actions:
- /integrity        search and highlight
- yy then p         copy and paste a line
- u and Ctrl-r      undo and redo
- :check            verify disk state
- :hash             show the full fingerprint
- ?                 open the quick reference
